門前Our name & the grill

“In front of the gate.”

Monzen (門前) means “before the gate”. In Japan, it's the street of small eateries outside a temple gate, where people stop to eat, drink and catch up. That's the feeling here: an easy, friendly stop for a good meal.

一

Fire first

Meat, seafood and vegetables cooked over high heat, so the outside chars and the inside stays juicy.

二

Salt or sauce

A pinch of salt to taste the produce, or a glossy tare glaze brushed on over the heat.

三

Straight to the table

Plates come out as they're ready. Order a few, share, then order a few more.
